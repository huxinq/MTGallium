# Security and publication privacy

Do not post leaked credentials, hidden game information, canonical replays,
unsafe artifact paths, or source/publication privacy defects in public issues.
The owner must establish a private reporting destination before publication;
until then, use the publication channel supplied by the owner rather than
inventing an address in this repository.

Reports should identify the affected public source revision, a minimal safe
reproduction, and whether the concern involves credentials, hidden information,
or an unsafe public artifact path.
