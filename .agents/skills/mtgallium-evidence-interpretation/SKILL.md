# Interpret retained MTGallium research

Start at the supplied result or run. For a project-level question, use the
research index supplied by private project context and follow the relevant links.

Recover the policy or model, learning target, input representation, source and
Argentum revisions, and material settings. When explaining a comparison, identify
what changed and which search, belief and rollout components the control shares.

For gameplay comparisons, recover setup groups and seat assignments before
aggregating outcomes. Inspect missing games, failures and exclusions where they
affect the denominator. For learning results, trace training and evaluation group
membership and any reuse of roots, branches or inspected labels. Read the target
construction when a claim depends on how search estimates or continuation results
became training examples.

Use retained numerical outputs to check the claimed comparison. Reuse established
verification; follow contradictions or missing support rather than rerunning the
experiment. Historical manifests use their producing revision's reader when byte
identity matters; [historical source](../../../docs/history.md) locates it.

Report the supported finding, evidence links and the limits that affect its
interpretation. Save it only when the task calls for a persistent result, in the
existing work note under [record organization](../../../docs/research-records.md).
